Served. Now come and eat it.

The Remetz burger, 220 g of chuck and brisket off the coals. It does not travel well. You should.

Nothing here touches a flat-top.

Every steak and every patty goes over oak and olive-wood coals, somewhere north of 400 °C. Remetz רֶמֶץ is the Hebrew word for the embers still glowing after the flame dies down. That is where the flavour is, so that is where we cook.

  • Beef dry-aged 45 days, on the bone
  • Chuck and brisket ground at 11:00, every day
  • Rested for as long as it was cooked

A room built around the grill.

Sixty seats, twelve of them at the counter facing the fire. Dark wood, low light, and the sound of fat hitting coals. Come for a burger at the bar or settle in for the tomahawk.
